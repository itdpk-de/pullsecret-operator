1) Define your registry credentials once, cluster-wide:

   apiVersion: pullsecret.itdpk.de/v1
   kind: ClusterPullSecret
   metadata:
     name: my-registry
   spec:
     credentials:
       secretRef:
         namespace: default
         name: my-docker-credentials
         key: .dockerconfigjson

2) Opt a workload in via annotation (comma-separate multiple ClusterPullSecrets):

   metadata:
     annotations:
       pullsecret.itdpk.de/cluster-pull-secrets: "my-registry"

   The operator creates a Secret named "pullsecret-<name>" in that workload's namespace.

3) Check status:

   kubectl get clusterpullsecrets
